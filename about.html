About NoirKit

NoirKit is a free Instagram username generator: you pick a vibe, add a keyword if you want one, and it produces handle ideas that follow Instagram's public username rules. No account, no credits, no waiting.

How it works

The generator combines style-specific word lists (dark, aesthetic, cute, gaming, tech, professional and more) with prefix, suffix and separator patterns, then filters every result against Instagram's format rules before showing it:

  • 1–30 characters
  • Letters, periods and underscores only
  • No trailing period and no two periods in a row
  • Must contain at least one letter
  • No duplicates within a batch

Generation uses a seeded, deterministic algorithm, which is why a batch with the same inputs and seed repeats exactly — and why fresh batches change when you hit regenerate.

What NoirKit does not do: it never checks whether a handle is actually free on Instagram. Availability changes constantly and can't be verified responsibly from a static tool, so every suggestion is presented as an idea — search it on Instagram before you claim it.

Privacy notice

NoirKit is a static website. There is no backend, no database and no account system.

  • Keywords and initials you type never leave your device — generation happens entirely in your browser.
  • Favorites are stored in your browser's local storage on this device only. Clearing site data removes them.
  • Analytics: none are installed. No cookies are set, no tracking pixels load, no third-party scripts run.
  • Hosting logs: the hosting provider records standard server logs (IP, user agent, requested path) for security and reliability, as most hosts do.
  • If analytics or advertising are ever added, they will be disclosed on this page first.
