      How to choose a memorable username

      Your handle is the one part of a profile people can't change often. A few rules that hold up:

      1. Keep it short enough to say out loud. Under about 15 characters is the sweet spot — longer handles get cut off in story mentions and tag lists.
      2. Pick one separator and stick with it. Mixing . and _ in the same handle is how people end up at the wrong account.
      3. Put the important word first. Instagram search weights the username heavily, so lead with your niche or name, not a filler prefix.
      4. Numbers are fine sparingly. mira.ceramics reads as a brand; mira.ceramics8472 reads as a bot. Use a meaningful number or none.
      5. Check it before you fall in love. Search the exact handle on Instagram — and check the profile URL — because suggestions here are ideas, not availability checks.
      6. Match it across platforms. The same handle everywhere makes you easy to find and hard to impersonate.

      Instagram usernames run 1–30 characters and may only contain letters, periods and underscores — no trailing period, no two periods in a row. Every suggestion here is filtered against those rules before it reaches you.

      Frequently asked questions

      Does this check if a username is available on Instagram?

      No. NoirKit generates creative, format-valid suggestions only. It never checks Instagram and never claims a handle is free — search for the exact name on Instagram before you register it.

      Is NoirKit free? Do I need an account?

      Completely free, no signup, no email, no credits. The generator runs entirely in your browser.

      What characters can Instagram usernames contain?

      Letters, numbers, periods and underscores, up to 30 characters. Usernames can't end with a period or contain two periods in a row. This generator filters suggestions against those rules.

      Can I use a keyword or my initials?

      Yes. Enter a hobby, mood, niche or name in the keyword field — the Niche style builds every suggestion around it. Initials get woven into suggestions too, for personal touches like ab.luna.

      Do you save my keywords or favorites?

      No servers are involved. Keywords stay in the page, and favorites are stored in your browser's local storage on this device only. See the privacy notice.

      How many usernames can I generate at once?

      Up to 40 per batch, with one-click copy for the whole batch, individual copy buttons, regenerate for a fresh batch, and local favorites for saving the keepers.

      Why did my favorite disappear?

      Favorites live in your browser's storage. Clearing site data or using a private window removes them. If a handle matters, copy it somewhere safe too.
